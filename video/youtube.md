## Description, as published

OpenStall Collective turns women who make and sell on WhatsApp into one supplier that any AI shopping agent can buy
from, in three steps: list, split, and pay on proof. Claude does the talking, code decides, and PayPal holds the money:
one AUTHORIZE order for the whole buy, and each maker's share is captured only when she proves she shipped.
