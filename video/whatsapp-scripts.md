# WhatsApp chats to animate

Exported from the app itself on November 11 (test mode on this computer; A0 from the hosted demo's real WhatsApp). Each message is word for word what OpenStall sends or receives. *In English* lines are for the viewer; the maker sees only her language. Buttons are WhatsApp quick replies. Order numbers differ on every run: use the one in the screen recording, or ord_demo. Receipt photos to show: assets/receipt-priya.jpg and assets/receipt-amina.jpg; her product photo: assets/sample-cushion-photo.png.
