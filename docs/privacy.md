# OpenStall Collective: privacy policy

OpenStall Collective is a demo built for the PayPal AI Hackathon by the Matrix-AE team. It runs on PayPal's sandbox, or in test mode on a simulated PayPal: **no real money moves**, and it is not a commercial service.

## What we collect

When you message OpenStall on WhatsApp, or use its web chat, we keep:

- your WhatsApp number and the name on your WhatsApp profile;
- the messages, photos and voice notes you send, and the text our speech service hears in your voice notes;
- the listing and limits you give us (your price, how many you can make, your city), and the orders you take part in.

## How we use it

Only to run the demo: to answer you in your language, turn your messages into a listing, tell you about orders, and record proof of shipping. Your voice notes are sent to ElevenLabs to be turned into text, and your photos and words to Anthropic's Claude to be read; replies are sent through WhatsApp (Meta or Twilio). Payments are PayPal sandbox payments, with no real money.

We do not sell your data, show you advertising, or share it with anyone else.

## How long we keep it

For as long as the demo runs, at the latest until the hackathon's winners are announced in December 2026. Then the demo's data is deleted.

## Deleting your data

Ask us to delete everything we hold about you at any time, by opening an issue at https://github.com/FahadRamxan/OpenStall-Collective/issues, and we delete it.
